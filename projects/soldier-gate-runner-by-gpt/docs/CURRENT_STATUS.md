## Goal
Deliver one complete, browser-playable round of an original squad gate-runner inspired by the visual language of Last War: Survival, without copying its assets. Priorities are clearly recognizable soldiers, continuous forward fire, real enemy casualties, substantial squad growth, arithmetic gate choices, shootable arithmetic targets, and a final commander. Detailed backgrounds are lower priority than readable figures and combat.

## Current Implementation
- Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript and original Canvas-drawn visuals. No copied Last War assets, external runtime libraries/CDNs, backend, accounts, persistence, or AI model.
- Touch/drag and arrow/A/D steering; fullscreen control; viewport-locked game screen without document-level scrolling. The upper-right toolbar contains Reset Run and ×1/×2/×3/×4 speed controls; the bottom road is no longer covered by a reset/message panel, and the in-world “YOUR SQUAD” plate is removed.
- Arithmetic gates support additive, multiplicative, percentage, and negative effects.
- Original blue allies and red enemies on a simplified road scene, with a bright blue/white/yellow arcade HUD. The outside field is now a clean blue gradient and the road is segmented gray with thin white edges and two perspective divider lines defining three lanes; backgrounds remain deliberately sparse.
- Allies are now rear-facing silhouettes with oversized helmets, segmented back packs, visible legs/boots, and rifles pointed forward; enemies face the player.
- The game canvas fills the entire browser viewport; the world width adapts to the viewport aspect ratio so there are no fixed-aspect letterbox bands. HUD and controls float above the playfield as compact overlays.
- The player's formation remains within one road lane and grows backward by adding rows. It uses four separated columns on desktop, with spacing and soldier scale adapted for narrow displays; drag steering moves continuously between lane centers, and fire shifts proportionally across adjacent lanes during transitions. At most 72 ally models are drawn at once because of available screen depth (enemy formations render up to 32); logical squad count and firing volume scale independently, with an internal numeric safety bound of 1,000,000,000 rather than a normal gameplay limit.
- Detailed soldier-model refinement remains a later task. Current original toy-soldier models are intentional interim placeholders while viewport layout, lane containment, and the core gameplay loop are reviewed.
- Continuous player fire uses fixed straight trajectories. Ordinary shots travel forward along their road-relative lane; first-contact, selected-target, and shootable-object shots choose a straight direction at launch and do not steer or register remote/teleport hits. Damage applies only when a projectile path physically intersects a drawn enemy, arithmetic target, barrel, or commander. Each newly visible enemy may receive a first-contact volley irrespective of squad lane; after the first hit, normal lane-forward fire resumes. Enemy squads and the commander fire visible red projectiles; an allied soldier is lost only when an enemy projectile physically hits the formation, replacing timer-based invisible losses. Enemy formation visuals scale down with casualties while the logical count remains available in the HUD. Clicking/tapping an enemy or shootable target locks it and steers toward its line of fire; drag steering remains available. A tank barrel takes 500 confirmed hits and grants 10 seconds of 4× damage against enemies and the commander. Normal run speed is 110 world units per second; ×1/×2/×3/×4 speed settings persist across Reset Run.
- Projectile origins now use the shared rendered-squad geometry. Unguided fire follows a normalized lane within the perspective road as the road narrows; aimed trajectories are tested from a large squad near the road edge. Projectile positions are checked against road margins. Muzzle flashes, tracer brightness, impact bursts, soldier equipment details and boss hit feedback were strengthened; gates and shootable targets spawn fully on the visible road.
- Boss health decreases from actual projectile hits, not passive elapsed time. Boss counterattack can reduce the player's squad.
- One shootable arithmetic target is now implemented: the ordinary split gate at that slot is replaced with a single blue `+1` target. Each real projectile hit increments the shown value, and crossing applies the final displayed value to squad count. Other split gates remain ordinary route-choice gates.
