## Scope Lock
- One continuous mission: 12 starting units, arithmetic-gate choices, three marked road lanes with continuous steering and weighted fire between adjacent lanes, continuous squad fire, 25 route events across three named sectors, six enemy archetypes with distinct movement/fire patterns, `+1` / `×2` / `+25%` shootable reward panels, optional tank-fire rewards, final commander, win/loss, reset and replay. This expands the mission, not the number of separate levels.
- Use original art; do not copy Last War assets/code. Do not add runtime libraries or backend.
- No additional levels, app-store submission, accounts, persistence, progression saves, monetization, multiplayer, or AI model.
- Prefer readable figures and satisfying combat over detailed backgrounds. Detailed soldier-modeling is a later milestone, after the viewport, three-lane continuous movement, weighted fire, formation behavior, and core gameplay loop are accepted.
- Squad totals should not feel artificially capped. Current internal numeric safety bound is 1,000,000,000; on-screen player formation renders up to 72 representative ally models (enemy formations up to 32) and responds to logical squad count; volleys scale with squad size up to 32 projectiles. Actual player perception of large-scale growth remains to be reviewed.
- Ordinary route-choice gates remain distinct from shootable arithmetic rewards. The mission now contains three variants (`+1`, `×2`, `+25%`), changed by physical hits and applied when crossed; tapping/clicking locks straight fire onto that lane. Two optional 500-hit tank barrels can each grant 10 seconds of 4× firepower. PR #219 is merged and LIVE; adjust balance only after normal-speed play review.
