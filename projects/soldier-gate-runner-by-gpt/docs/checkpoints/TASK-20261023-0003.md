## Scope Lock
- One round only: 12 starting units, left/right arithmetic-gate choices, three marked road lanes with continuous steering and weighted fire between adjacent lanes, continuous squad fire, three enemy waves, final commander, win/loss, reset and replay.
- Use original art; do not copy Last War assets/code. Do not add runtime libraries or backend.
- No additional levels, app-store submission, accounts, persistence, progression saves, monetization, multiplayer, or AI model.
- Prefer readable figures and satisfying combat over detailed backgrounds. Detailed soldier-modeling is a later milestone, after the viewport, three-lane continuous movement, weighted fire, formation behavior, and core gameplay loop are accepted.
- Squad totals should not feel artificially capped. Current internal numeric safety bound is 1,000,000,000; on-screen player formation renders up to 72 representative ally models (enemy formations up to 32) and responds to logical squad count; volleys scale with squad size up to 32 projectiles. Actual player perception of large-scale growth remains to be reviewed.
- Ordinary route-choice gates remain distinct from shootable arithmetic targets. Current shootable target is a positive add target: each confirmed hit increments `+1 → +2 → +3...`; the last displayed amount is applied at crossing. More target types are not yet implemented.
